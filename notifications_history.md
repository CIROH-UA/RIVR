| 2026-08-22 | (this deploy) | publish-cadence-probe.ts (new), index.ts, noaa-client.ts | **ADR 0011 Phase 0 instrumentation — not a notification change.** New 1st-gen function `probePublishCadence` (us-central1, 256MB/180s, hourly `0 * * * *` UTC) samples NOAA's unfiltered `/streamflow` for one reach and records each series' `referenceTime`, elapsed ms, bytes and HTTP status to a new `publish_cadence_log` collection. | 2026-08-22 | (this deploy) | publish-cadence-probe.ts, publish-cadence-probe.test.ts | **ADR 0011 Phase 0 probe v2 — samples all five endpoints, not just the heaviest.** v1 measured only the unfiltered `/streamflow` response. | 2026-08-29 | 89a56b6, 5a97e61 | store-trigger.ts, store-service.ts, store-window.ts | **ADR 0011 Phase 7 monitoring — not a notification change.** Whole `default` codebase redeployed twice in one sitting. Health was measured across the WHOLE `river_data` collection via `lastSuccessfulWrite`, so one fresh write reported the entire store healthy and a product that stopped being written was invisible. `assessProductFreshness` now judges the newest document per product against that product's `MAX_HOLD_MS`. **The first deploy false-alarmed within a minute:** `storeHealth` returned 503 `{"status":"down","problems":["returnPeriods has not advanced for 17h (cap 6h)","reachMetadata has not advanced for 17h (cap 6h)"]}` on a healthy store — the two near-static products have no refresh cycle, hold a 30-day window and are rewritten only when missing or nearly expiring, but had no `MAX_HOLD_MS` entry and inherited the 6-hour default meant for hourly forecasts.
